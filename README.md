# SurakshaAR
AR-Based Vocational Training Simulator for Industrial Safety in Jharkhand's Mining &amp; Manufacturing Sector
Suraksha AR is an application/system where AR technology overlays safety information (like warnings, directions, instructions) on the real world using a mobile phone or AR device.
👉 Example: When you point your phone camera at a place, it shows emergency exits, hazard zones, or safety instructions on the screen.
